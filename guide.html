Read my Bible

A free Bible reading game for ages 4–12. Together, we’re reading the Book of Matthew, one chapter a day for 28 days!

WHAT KIDS DO EACH DAY

Read. Answer. Fly!

READOne chapter. Ages 4–6: one verse.
ANSWER3 quick questions.
FLYThe Sky Run game unlocks!

Every day they read, their streak fire grows, they unlock new outfits, and their home grows from a tent to a mansion. They can also send you a voice message or prayer.
